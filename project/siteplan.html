WDD131 Site Plan

Site Name

Cool Car Academy

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to educate vehicle owners about automotive air conditioning systems. It provides easy-to-understand information about how the system works, its main components, common problems, maintenance tips, and best practices to help users keep their vehicle's A/C in good condition and make informed decisions before visiting a repair shop.

Scenarios

  • What are the main components of a vehicle's air conditioning system, and what does each one do?
  • How often should a vehicle's A/C system be serviced?
  • What are the signs that my vehicle's A/C system needs professional attention?
  • Can I damage my vehicle's A/C system by ignoring small problems?
  • What is refrigerant, and why is it important for the A/C system?
